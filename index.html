04 · Chip & GPU guide

🧠 Silicon, honestly — two naming traps inside these listings

Dedicated GPUs dominate gaming and editing; integrated ones win on battery and weight. Know which is which before paying.

The Aspire 7 CPUs — with My prices

i7-13620H10C (6P+4E) / 16T — strongest AND cheapest → the default at ₹64,809
Core 7 240H8C / 12T — “i7 14th Gen” is a sticker, not a stronger chip → ₹75,449
Core 5 210H8C / 12T — same layout as the i5-13420H → ₹69,000 with the 4GB card

The Slim 3 15AHP10 trap

Ryzen 7 7735HS8C/16T + Radeon 680M → real iGPU gaming. Unconfirmed for this listing.
Ryzen 7 7435HS8C/16T + Radeon 610M ≈ Intel UHD class → the likely case the ranking assumes.

Windows-on-Arm: what runs on Snapdragon X

  • ✅ Native & fast: VS Code, Visual Studio, Python, Node.js, Java, Android Studio, browsers, MS Office
  • ⚠️ Emulated via Prism — usually fine, occasionally slow: most other x86 apps
  • ❌ Often blocked: anti-cheat games (Valorant), Docker x86, some pro video tools, older utilities & drivers

Applies to the Acer Aspire 14 AI A14-11M and Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 15Q8X10. Verify My course software and games before choosing Arm.

Dedicated vs integrated

  • RTX tier (5 listings): real 1080p gaming + CUDA/NVENC editing. Costs: weight, battery, usually no Office bundle.
  • iGPU tier: fine for college and coding; esports at low settings at best.
