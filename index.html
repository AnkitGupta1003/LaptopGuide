          ✅ What the photos don't show — verify before you buy

          Listing images hide the details that decide whether you'll love or regret a laptop. Check these on the spec sheet / reviews before hitting Buy.

          🧩RAM type

          DDR4 vs DDR5 — DDR5 is meaningfully faster. HP shows DDR5; the others don't say. Also check if there's a free slot for upgrades.

          🔆Display quality

          Brightness (250 vs 300 nits), colour gamut, refresh rate. "FHD" alone tells you nothing about how it actually looks.

          🔋Battery

          Wh capacity and real-world backup. "Fast Charge" (HP) is nice, but capacity matters more for full college days.

          ⚖️Weight & build

          Metal vs plastic body, thickness. You'll carry this daily — 1.6 kg vs 1.9 kg is a real difference.

          🔌Ports

          USB-C with charging? HDMI? SD card slot (editors, take note)? Check before you buy dongles forever.

          🛡️Warranty type

          On-site vs carry-in — and what "1Y Accidental Damage Protection" actually covers. Big deal for hostel life.

          📄MS Office license

          Lifetime license vs 1-year subscription — very different value. Ask which one ships with it.

          🔧Upgradability

          Extra RAM slot or second SSD slot? A ₹57K laptop with an open slot ages much better.

          🌀Thermals & noise

          How loud does it get under compile/gaming load? Check YouTube reviews of the exact model.

          🏭Service centres near you

          Lenovo & HP have India's widest networks; Acer/ASUS are decent; Motorola's laptop service is still new.

          💡 Pro tip: if gaming is a serious priority

          Consider stretching to an RTX 4050 laptop — Lenovo LOQ, HP Victus or ASUS TUF — which often hit ₹70–80K during Flipkart sales, right in this budget. It would beat all 12 of these at gaming AND editing by a mile. The rest of your needs (coding, college) are already covered by any laptop on this page.
