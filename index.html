          🧠 Chip & GPU Guide — corrected

          Two naming traps live in these listings. Both are flagged below, with the v3 corrections applied.

          The Aspire 7 CPUs — and the "i7 14th Gen" trap

          i7-13620H10C (6P+4E) / 16T ✅ — the strongest Aspire 7 chip here
          Core 5 210H8C (4P+4E) / 12T ✅ — same layout as the i5-13420H (rebrand tier)
          Core 7 240H8C (4P+4E) / 12T ✅ — v2 wrongly said 16T. "i7 14th Gen" is Acer marketing: it's a Core Series-1 (Raptor Lake refresh) part, weaker than the 13620H. Verify on Intel ARK.

          The Slim 3 15AHP10 trap

          Ryzen 7 7735HS8C/16T + Radeon 680M → real iGPU gaming. Unconfirmed for this listing.
          Ryzen 7 7435HS8C/16T + Radeon 610M ≈ Intel UHD class → likely for this chassis — the conservative case v3 ranks on.

          Windows-on-Arm: what runs on Snapdragon X

          • ✅ Native & fast: VS Code, Visual Studio, Python, Node.js, Java, Android Studio, browsers, MS Office
          • ⚠️ Emulated via Prism — usually fine, occasionally slow: most other x86 apps
          • ❌ Often blocked: anti-cheat games (Valorant), Docker x86, some pro video tools, older drivers/utilities

          Applies to Acer Aspire 14 AI A14-11M and Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 15Q8X10. Verify your course software + games before choosing Arm — regardless of price.

          Dedicated vs integrated — the honest gap

          • RTX tier (5 listings): real 1080p gaming + CUDA/NVENC editing. Costs: weight, battery, usually no Office bundle.
          • iGPU tier: fine for college/coding; esports at low settings at best.
