          ⚠️ The 7435HS trap: PSREF confirms 7735HS + 680M in the Slim 3 family — but the matched unit was 16-inch. Your 15.3" 15AHP10 chassis is widely sold in India with the 7435HS, whose Radeon 610M is no better than Intel UHD. Same 8-core CPU either way; the GPU is the whole difference.

          Windows-on-Arm: what runs on the Snapdragon

          • ✅ Native & fast: VS Code, Visual Studio, Python, Node.js, Java, Android Studio, browsers, MS Office, Spotify
          • ⚠️ Emulated via Prism — usually fine, occasionally slow: most other x86/x64 apps
          • ❌ Often blocked: anti-cheat games (Valorant), Docker x86 images, some pro video tools, older utilities & drivers

          If gaming or specific engineering software is on your list, verify each one before choosing the Aspire 14 AI — regardless of how good the price is.
